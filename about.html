Meet Taylor

My passion is helping people feel stronger and more confident in their bodies through Pilates. I began my practice in 2018, searching for a form of movement I’d genuinely enjoy and want to keep coming back to, and I found it. After five years in corporate real estate, I made the leap to teaching Pilates full time. Since completing my training, I’ve taught more than 2,500 classes.

I blend a Classical foundation with a contemporary approach, drawing on my Balanced Body Comprehensive training across the full range of Pilates apparatus: Mat, Reformer, Cadillac/Trapeze Table, Chair, and Barrels. That versatility lets me meet you where you are and build sessions that are well-rounded, challenging, and tailored to your goals.

I believe form is the foundation of every Pilates practice. In private sessions, I spend a lot of time fine-tuning your form, working toward unconscious competence: the point where good movement becomes second nature. Once you’ve mastered the fundamentals, I begin adding layers, props, and progressions so you can experience the same movement with new feedback. My group classes follow the same philosophy. About 80% of each class is familiar movement that builds mastery, and up to 20% is a new challenge, whether that’s a new exercise, a new prop, or a new breathing technique.

I work with clients looking to deepen their practice or move into advanced work, new instructors seeking a mentor, and home reformer owners who want expert guidance. I’ve also spent hundreds of hours working one-on-one with clients with scoliosis, and I’m confident using the entire Pilates system to support their unique bodies. After completing specialty Pre & Postnatal training with Bodyline in 2025, I can support clients at every stage of that journey. Additional training in the Oov and Wellround Myofascial Release rounds out a truly holistic, personalized approach to your wellness.

When I’m not teaching, you’ll usually find me with a good book, cooking, spending time with my husband and our black lab, or learning even more about Pilates.
