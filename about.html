Meet Taylor

My passion is helping people feel stronger and more confident in their bodies through Pilates. I blend a Classical foundation with a contemporary approach, drawing on my Balanced Body Comprehensive training across the full range of Pilates apparatus: Mat, Reformer, Cadillac/Trapeze Table, Chair, and Barrels. That versatility lets me meet you where you are and build sessions that are well-rounded, challenging, and tailored to your goals.

My specialized training in Pre & Postnatal Pilates, the Oov, and Wellround Myofascial Release allows me to take a truly holistic, personalized approach to your wellness.
