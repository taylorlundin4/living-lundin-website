Privacy Policy

Last updated November 17, 2026

Living Lundin is a private Pilates business run by Taylor. This page explains what information this website collects and how it’s used. The short version: very little, and nothing is sold or shared.

What this website collects

This website has no sign-up forms, no accounts, no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools. It doesn’t set cookies.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and its fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. Like most web services, they may keep standard technical logs, such as your IP address and browser type, to deliver and secure the site. Their handling of that data is covered by the GitHub Privacy Statement and the Google Privacy Policy.

When you email me

If you email me, I receive what you choose to send, such as your name, email address, goals, equipment, schedule and location. I use it only to reply to you, schedule and plan sessions, and stay in touch about your sessions. I don’t sell, rent or share it with anyone for marketing.

My email is provided by Google (Gmail), so your messages are stored with them.

Links to other sites

This site links to Instagram, Pinterest, YouTube and ShopMy. Those sites have their own privacy policies, which apply once you visit them.

Your choices

You can ask me to see, correct or delete any information you’ve sent me by emailing taylorlundin4@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
