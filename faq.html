Frequently asked Q’s

What equipment is required?

Sessions are fully customized to the apparatus and props you have at home, from a mat to a full reformer, tower or chair. Reformers must be from a professional-grade brand such as Balanced Body, Merrithew, Peak Pilates or Your Reformer.

If you have a custom setup or don’t see your equipment listed, please reach out to see how we may be able to work together.

What is your pricing?
  • In-person session: starting at $120
  • Virtual session: starting at $75
  • Workshops & events: inquire for pricing
What areas do you serve?

In-home sessions are available in Bellevue, Issaquah and North Bend on Seattle’s Eastside. Virtual sessions are available anywhere.

How can I schedule with you?

We currently work on a referral-only basis to keep every session high quality and personal. Join the waitlist to hear the moment a spot opens for new clients.

What training do you have?

A Balanced Body Comprehensive Pilates certification and a Classical Pilates certification in Mat and Reformer through EYT, plus specialty training in pre and postnatal Pilates. I’m also Oov certified and certified in Wellround Myofascial Release.

Are you insured?

Yes. A certificate of insurance is available on request.
